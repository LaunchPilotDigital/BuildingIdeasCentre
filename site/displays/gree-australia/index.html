Gree Australia

Did you know Gree is the number one brand of air conditioners world-wide?

Gree is the number one brand of air conditioners in the world. One in three air conditioning units is produced by Gree Electric, and there are over 400 million users worldwide. Gree has been distributed in Australia for over 20 years with over 300 dealers nationwide and is distributed through over seventy Kirby and Beijer Ref Australia distribution outlets across the country.

Gree products come with a comprehensive 6-year parts and labour warranty. Gree is supported locally by a full range of spare parts and is backed by a skilled technical team and warranty providers for comprehensive after sales support.
