Touch Handles

The finishing touch | Jewellery for your cabinetry | Dress your home in detail | Quiet luxury, within reach | From our family to your front door

Touch Handles is a family-owned Australian brand creating cabinetry hardware that’s as beautiful as it is functional. Founded in 1996 when John began handcrafting ring-pull handles in his shed, they have since grown into a trusted name for designers, joiners, and renovators. From hand-finished bronze to Australian-made timber and hand-crafted marble designs, every piece is jewellery for the home, small, but full of impact. With sustainable practices, and a human touch in every detail, Touch Handles brings lasting style and quality to every space.
