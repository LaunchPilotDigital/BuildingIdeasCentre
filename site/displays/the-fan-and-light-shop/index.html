The Fan & Light Shop

Stylish, efficient, smart air movement & lighting for Australian homes

The Fan & Light Shop are specialists in ceiling fans, lighting & ventilation products designed specifically for Australian living. We offer a wide collection of products from energy efficient ceiling fans, outdoor fans as well as smart fans and lights. We also showcase a wide selection of decorative light fittings and the newest in LED lighting. Our air movement range also extends to ventilation products including domestic bathroom extractor fan & heaters units, and large commercial exhaust fans. We also offer consulting services with our highly skilled team from minor renovations, full home lighting plans, or large scale commercial projects.
