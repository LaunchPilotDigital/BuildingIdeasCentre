Kitchen Scene

Kitchen Cabinets, Design, Manufacture & Installation

With over 30 years of experience in the kitchen renovation industry, Kitchen Scene combined with sister company Town & Country Cabinets, has become one of South Australia’s leading and most respected designers and manufacturers of kitchens, laundries, alfresco, and all types of custom built joinery.

Our expert kitchen designers offer a free in home consultation to discuss your every need, and then work with you as your personal project manager to ensure your dream kitchen becomes a reality, on time and within budget, with no hidden surprises.

Using only the highest quality materials and hardware, all Kitchen Scene products are hand crafted at our state of the art manufacturing facility, ensuring a high quality product at a factory direct price.
