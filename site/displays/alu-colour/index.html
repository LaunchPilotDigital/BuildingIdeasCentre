Alu Colour

Re-Colour Aluminium Windows and Doors

ALU Colour was founded in 2016, with the aim to provide a solution to damaged, faded and outdated windows and door frames in South Australia. Before ALU Colour the only option was to replace your windows and door frames. Using our knowledge and experience in our sister company Adelaide Powder Coat repair we created a process to re-spray windows and doors to entire homes and shop fronts. At ALU colour we pride ourselves on the quality and finishes of our work, with the end result looking as good as new replicating the finished look of a new powder coat. Our team takes great pride in their work and are respectful of your home, completing the task at hand with minimal disruption. We offer the only quality professional on-site colour change solution to renovators and businesses.
