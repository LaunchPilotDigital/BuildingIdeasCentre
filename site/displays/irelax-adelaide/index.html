Irelax Adelaide

Massage Chairs & Bedding

For us, relaxation is a priority. It always has been in times like ours. Despite having better living conditions than we did in the past, it feels like people have less time for themselves, and more stress by the day. It does not always have to be this way though.

On a trip to Asia, our founder had the chance to experience a massage chair in the middle of a mall. His expectations were not high but was blown away by the experience. It was the beginning of a revelation. How come massage chairs were not as common here in Australia? Should we not be able to relax as well? From there, well, the rest is history.
