TF Feature Wall

South Australia’s Feature Wall & Venetian Plaster Specialists

TF Feature Wall brings centuries-old Venetian Plaster techniques to South Australian homes and businesses. From classic Marmorino and polished plaster to waterproof Microcement for bathrooms — every finish is custom designed and handcrafted. No two walls are ever the same.

We are Adelaide’s dedicated specialists in decorative venetian plaster and feature walls, working with architects, interior designers, builders, and homeowners who want something truly unique. Our Venetian plaster Adelaide clients include residential renovations, new builds, restaurants, hotels, and commercial fitouts across South Australia.
