Stylux Interiors

Transforming residential and commercial spaces through bespoke interior design.

At Stylux Interiors, we create spaces that are as functional as they are beautiful, blending timeless design with modern sophistication.

Our studio specialises in full-service interior design for residential and boutique commercial projects, offering bespoke solutions that reflect each client’s individuality. From concept to completion, every detail is carefully considered such as materials, light, texture, and form — to craft interiors that feel effortlessly refined and deeply personal.

Our Services Include:

  • Full-service interior design for new builds and renovations
  • Kitchen, bathroom, and joinery design
  • Space planning and concept development
  • Material and finish selections
  • Colour consultation and visualisation
  • Display home and boutique commercial interiors
