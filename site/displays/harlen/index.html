HARLEN

Handcrafted freestanding timber bathroom vanities.

Handcrafted freestanding timber bathroom vanities, where timeless elegance meets artisanal craftsmanship. Manufactured by a dedicated team led by Skills Gold Medallist and World Skills Finalist in Joinery and Wood machining, using FSC Certified American White Oak – Harlen vanities are setting new industry standards in craftsmanship, sustainability and social impact.

Every detail is purposefully considered: sustainably crafted and finished, Blum soft-close drawer hardware with a lifetime guarantee and all anchored by a meaningful social impact story that gives each piece even greater purpose.

Available in 5 standard sizes: 600, 750, 900, 1200, 1500.

Finish options: Natural American Oak, Tobacco Oak or any colour from the OSMO Elements colour range, which can be viewed via the website.

Elevate your bathroom renovation with a vanity that’s as refined as it is personal and crafted to stand the test of time.
