1KOMMA5°

1KOMMA5° Adelaide is the Heartbeat of New Energy.

1KOMMA5° Adelaide (formerly Kozco Energy Group) is revolutionising home energy with cutting-edge solar, battery, and EV charging solutions. As part of a global movement towards carbon-neutral living, they provide a range of high-performance residential and commercial energy solutions, including solar panels, smart battery storage, EV charging, smart home options and more. With expert installation and seamless integration, 1KOMMA5° Adelaide helps homeowners take control of their power, reduce reliance on the grid, and future-proof their energy needs. Backed by innovative technology and a commitment to sustainability, 1KOMMA5° Adelaide is driving the transition to cleaner, smarter energy.
